# Fafa Desktop Pet v0.2.0

Fafa has more to do! Play now switches between two paw poses, Feed includes a lip-lick, and Nap moves through a lounging pose before settling into closed-eye sleep. New **Stretch** and **Hide** buttons show Fafa in a deep bow stretch and peeking out of a cardboard box, using edits based on her photos. She also occasionally looks up or stretches on her own.

Pet her four times in quick succession for a cheeky looking-up reaction.
Waking her from a nap now pauses on the lounging pose for a sleepy complaint.

Download the portable Windows executable below and open it; no installation is needed. This build is unsigned, so Windows may show an unrecognized-app warning.
